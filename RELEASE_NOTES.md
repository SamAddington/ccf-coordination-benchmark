# CCF Coordination Benchmark v1.0.0 — release notes

Initial public-release candidate packaging of the CCF v26 synthetic coordination study (24 November 2026).

Included:
- 4,320 historical exploratory episodes; 2,592 primary fallback/placement episodes; 1,680 sensitivity episodes
- Complete nine-arm × four loss-level × two-deadline primary CSV, 72 condition summaries, world-cluster intervals, and 56 paired contrasts
- Unmodified source modules and generated plots supporting the reported simulation
- MIT grant covering original project code, author-generated synthetic datasets, and figures
- Reproduction command-line tooling, GitHub Actions smoke checks, citation metadata, notices, and SHA-256 manifest

Scientific limitations: synthetic author-defined workload and network; unpriced ideal local capacity guard; no physical network evaluation; no independent third-party reproduction. An author-reported Ubuntu/WSL2 rerun previously matched the 8,592 archived episodes.
